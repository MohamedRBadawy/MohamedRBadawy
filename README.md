### Mohammed Badawy

I find the manual, slow and duplicated parts of an operation and replace them with systems that keep running.

For over three years I have built operational systems for a manufacturer, fully remote, across sales, HR, finance, support and reporting. Before that, I ran my own delivery business for four years. I build by directing AI coding tools on a React, Node.js and MySQL stack, and I document what I build so others can run it.

**Most of my work is private, because it belongs to my employer.** The repositories here are built from scratch on invented data, to show how I work.
